Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PRP therapy?
PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) uses growth factors from your own blood to stimulate hair follicles and skin rejuvenation. It is natural and minimally invasive.
Is PRP effective for hair loss?
Yes — most patients see noticeable reduction in hair fall and visible regrowth across 3–6 sessions when paired with a tailored plan.
How many sessions are needed?
Typically 4–6 PRP sessions spaced 3–4 weeks apart, with maintenance sessions as advised by DermaCare Doctor.
Can acne scars be treated?
Yes. We use a combination of targeted therapies to progressively smooth pitted, rolling and boxcar scars.
What skin conditions do you treat?
Acne, pigmentation, melasma, fungal infections, eczema, psoriasis, allergies, rashes and itching, among others.
What is cupping therapy?
Cupping (Hijama) is a therapeutic technique that improves circulation, relieves pain and supports natural detoxification. At DermaCare it is performed in a sterile setting under the care of our MD dermatologist.
How long does a treatment take?
Most consultations take 20–30 minutes. Sessions range 30–60 minutes depending on the treatment.
Is consultation available for both men and women?
Yes — we treat patients of all ages and genders in a private, respectful environment.
